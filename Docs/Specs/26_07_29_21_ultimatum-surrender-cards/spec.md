# Spec: Ultimatum / Surrender Cards

## Feature Intent

As a player, I want to play an "Ultimatum" or "Surrender" action card on a country currently locked in a war, so that I have a direct way to force that war to a decisive end — Ultimatum forcing the selected country to a win, Surrender forcing it to a loss — once I hold enough sway over that country's military leadership and the war is already trending the right way, instead of every war only ever ending through the unconditional debug stop-war command.

## Acceptance Criteria

Legend: `Precondition => Action => Outcome`, grouped under a shared precondition where one applies to several rows.

- The selected country is not currently in a war.
  - The deck/draw system evaluates a free hand slot for this country => neither "Ultimatum" nor "Surrender" is eligible to be drawn.
  - A copy of either card is already sitting in hand and the country's war ends (naturally, via a debug stop-war, or via the other card of this pair being played first) => that copy is shown unplayable via the same conditions-gated check every other card already goes through; it stays in hand, it is never discarded or removed for this reason.
- The selected country is currently in a war, and the playing org's control in that country is at least 10, and the org's opinion with that country's military advisor is at least 50, and that country's own war progress (see Tech Notes for how "own" is measured) is at least 50.
  - The deck/draw system evaluates a free hand slot => "Ultimatum" is eligible to be drawn like any other country card, subject to the normal per-slot draw fill — there is no "push into hand the instant the condition becomes true" mechanic; eligibility is only re-checked the next time a slot is actually being filled.
  - A copy is in hand and its 300-gold cost is affordable => the card is shown as playable.
  - Any one of the four conditions above stops holding (control drops below 10, opinion drops below 50, war progress drops below 50, or the war ends) while a copy already sits in hand => it is shown unplayable via the same conditions-gated check; it is not removed from hand.
- The selected country is currently in a war, and the playing org's control in that country is at least 20, and the org's opinion with that country's military advisor is at least 80, and that country's own war progress is at least 0.
  - Same draw/play/unplayable-on-condition-drop behavior as "Ultimatum" above, but for "Surrender" with its own 500-gold cost.
- Both "Ultimatum" and "Surrender" are simultaneously eligible for the same country (e.g. control 25, opinion 90, war progress 60).
  - Hand/deck are evaluated => both cards are independently eligible and can both be drawn/held at the same time; playing one does not require or block the other.
- The player plays "Ultimatum" on a country that is playable (in a war, thresholds met, 300 gold paid).
  - The play resolves => always succeeds, no roll of any kind; the war the selected country is currently fighting ends immediately with the selected country as the winner and its opponent as the loser; the card leaves the hand and a replacement is drawn into the vacated slot as usual.
- The player plays "Surrender" on a country that is playable (in a war, thresholds met, 500 gold paid).
  - The play resolves the same way, except the selected country ends the war as the loser and its opponent as the winner.
- The player opens the selected country's Actions panel with either card in hand or in the deck pile, at any time.
  - Neither card ever shows a cooldown label, and playing one copy never blocks another copy of the same card, or the sibling card, from being drawn or played — consistent with the project-wide removal of the cooldown mechanic.
- The player views the Action Log after playing either card.
  - One entry appears reporting the war's outcome: which country won and which lost.

## Tech Notes

- **War lookup and resolution — new capability, nothing like it exists today.** `src/Game.Systems/Wars.cs` currently only offers `IsInWar`, `DeclareWar`, and `StopWar` (a hard delete with no outcome recorded — confirmed by `Docs/Specs/26_07_25_06_war-mechanics-core/spec.md`'s own Tech Notes and Out of Scope: "no negotiated end, no victory/defeat determination"). Add:
  - New `enum WarOutcome { Win, Lose }` in `src/Game.Common/WarOutcome.cs`, sibling to `WarParticipantKind.cs`/`RelationKind.cs` — describes the outcome *for the country the card was played on*, not an absolute attacker/defender label.
  - New `Wars.ResolveWar(World world, string countryId, WarOutcome outcomeForCountry) : bool` in `src/Game.Systems/Wars.cs`. Finds the `WarParticipant` entity for `countryId` (same scan `StopWar` already does); if none, returns `false` (no-op — the conditions gate below should make this unreachable via normal play, matching `StopWar`'s own not-in-war no-op). Otherwise reads that entity's `WarId`, finds the opponent's `WarParticipant` entity (same `WarId`, other `CountryId`), computes `winnerCountryId`/`loserCountryId` from `countryId`/opponent id and `outcomeForCountry`, then destroys the `War`+`WarProgress` entity and both `WarParticipant` entities for that `WarId` — the same hard-delete `StopWar` performs. Consider extracting the "find the war + both participant entities by one country id" scan that both `StopWar` and `ResolveWar` need into one shared private helper in `Wars.cs`, rather than duplicating it.
  - Emits a new sibling Game Log entity `WarResolvedApplied { WarId, WinnerCountryId, LoserCountryId }` after the destroy, following the same "separate sibling entity, not attached to the effect component" convention `DiscoverCountrySystem`/`SetCountryRelationSystem`/`ClearCountryRelationSystem` already use for their own applied-events. New `GameLogLineFormatter` branch + `game_log.war_resolved_format` locale key (e.g. "{0} won the war against {1}"), resolved with the winner's and loser's `country_name.*` values, following the existing `string.Format(_loc.Get(key), ...)` pattern.
- **"Own" war progress — a new, direction-aware reading of `WarProgress.Value`, resolved per selected country, not raw.** `WarSystem.Update` (`src/Game.Systems/WarSystem.cs`) only ever *decreases* `WarProgress.Value` (the attacker-side monthly decay), so `Value`'s sign convention is attacker-relative: positive means the attacker currently holds the advantage, negative means the defender does. Since these two cards gate on "the selected country's own progress" rather than the raw attacker-relative number, resolve a signed per-country value: if the selected country's `WarParticipant.Kind == Attacker`, use `WarProgress.Value` directly; if `Defender`, use `-WarProgress.Value`. This is naturally bounded to `[0, 100]` on the defender's side today (since `Value` is floor-clamped at `-100` by `WarSystem.Update` and nothing currently raises it above `0`), and to `[-100, 0]` on the attacker's side — no new clamp is needed. **Known consequence, called out for visibility, not treated as a bug:** because nothing in the codebase currently *increases* `WarProgress.Value` (natural war progress is out of scope of `Docs/Specs/26_07_25_06_war-mechanics-core/spec.md`), an attacking country's own signed progress can only ever fall over time, so in practice today "Ultimatum" (needs own progress ≥ 50) is realistically reachable only by the defending side of a war, not the attacking side, until a future feature adds a way to raise progress.
- **New DSL nodes** in `src/Game.Configs/ExpressionNode.cs`: add `IsInWar` and `WarProgress` (both `double`) to `ExpressionContext`, plus `case "isInWar": return ctx.IsInWar;` and `case "warProgress": return ctx.WarProgress;` in `Evaluate`. Both new fields must be populated, alongside the existing `Control`/`TotalCountryControl`/`Opinion` fields, at every country-card `ExpressionContext` construction site: `src/Game.Systems/ActionPlayability.cs` (`Evaluate`), `src/Game.Systems/DrawCardSystem.cs` (`DrawCountryCards`), `src/Game.Main/VisualStateConverter.cs` (`BuildEntry`), and `InitSystem.CreateCountryActionEntities` if that call site also builds a context for initial-hand eligibility (matches the same four-site pattern `Docs/Specs/26_07_29_00_decrease-enemy-control-card/spec.md`'s Tech Notes used for `TotalCountryControl`). Resolve both via `Wars.IsInWar(world, countryId)` and the new "own war progress" helper above (default `IsInWar = 0`, `WarProgress = 0` when not in a war — harmless, since `IsInWar` is always checked explicitly by both cards' conditions rather than relying on a default).
- **Opinion-at-role must become role-aware — a real, pre-existing gap this feature needs closed.** Today, `Opinion` is resolved identically at all three/four call sites via a hardcoded `"diplomacy_advisor"` role string (`CharacterQuery.GetTargetCharacterByCountryAndRole(world, countryId, "diplomacy_advisor")` then `ResourceQuery.GetValue(world, charId, $"opinion_{orgId}")`), independent of the card's own `ActionDefinition.TargetRole`. No existing card gates its conditions on `opinion` with a non-`diplomacy_advisor` `TargetRole` today, so this has never surfaced as a bug. Fix: at each of those call sites, resolve the character via `def.TargetRole` instead of the literal `"diplomacy_advisor"`. This is behavior-preserving for every existing card (`make_friend`, `make_rival`, `stop_friendship`, `stop_rivalry` all already have `TargetRole = "diplomacy_advisor"`; cards with `TargetRole = ""`, e.g. `sphere_of_pressure`/`decrease_enemy_control`, resolve no character and get `Opinion = 0`, same as today since none of their conditions reference `opinion`), and is what lets "Ultimatum"/"Surrender" (`TargetRole = "military_advisor"`) gate `opinion` on the country's military advisor instead.
- **New config rows in `Assets/Configs/action_config.json`** (`ActionDefinition`, `src/Game.Configs/ActionConfig.cs`), both `ownerType: "country"`, `rarity: "Standard"`, `targetRole: "military_advisor"`, `deckCopies: 3` (matching the convention every other country card already uses — the issue doesn't specify a copy count):
  - `ultimatum`: `cost: [{ "resourceId": "gold", "amount": 300.0 }]`, `conditions: [gte(control, 10), gte(opinion, 50), gte(isInWar, 1), gte(warProgress, 50)]` (four independent entries, AND'd together the same way `ActionPlayability.Evaluate`/`DrawCardSystem` already AND every entry in `Conditions`), `effectIds: ["ultimatum_effect"]`.
  - `surrender`: same shape, `cost: [{ "resourceId": "gold", "amount": 500.0 }]`, `conditions: [gte(control, 20), gte(opinion, 80), gte(isInWar, 1), gte(warProgress, 0)]`, `effectIds: ["surrender_effect"]`.
  - Neither carries a success-rate/roll field of any kind — no such field exists on `ActionDefinition` today, and "always succeeds" is already every other roll-free card's (e.g. `make_friend`, `decrease_enemy_control`) default with no field present.
- **New effect type** `ResolveWarEffectParams : ActionEffectDefinition { public WarOutcome Outcome { get; set; } }` in `src/Game.Configs/EffectConfig.cs`, registered as `case "ResolveWar":` in `ActionEffectDefinitionListConverter`'s switch (alongside the existing `ControlChange`/`OpinionModifier`/`SetCountryRelation`/`ClearCountryRelation`/`EnemyControlDrain` cases). Two new rows in `Assets/Configs/effect_config.json`: `ultimatum_effect` (`effectType: "ResolveWar"`, `outcome: "Win"`), `surrender_effect` (`effectType: "ResolveWar"`, `outcome: "Lose"`).
- **Dispatch — inline, no marker entity, no new system, no `GameLogic.cs` orchestration change**, following the same reasoning `Docs/Specs/26_07_29_00_decrease-enemy-control-card/spec.md` used for its drain effect: resolving and destroying the war/participant entities needs no `Random` and no data `CreateActionEffectSystem` doesn't already have easy access to via a plain query helper. Add `else if (effectDef is ResolveWarEffectParams resolveWarParams && !string.IsNullOrEmpty(countryId)) { Wars.ResolveWar(world, countryId, resolveWarParams.Outcome); }` to `CreateActionEffectSystem.Update`'s effect dispatch (`src/Game.Systems/CreateActionEffectSystem.cs`). `Wars.cs` is a plain static helper (no `Update` entry point, not a "System"), so this call does not violate `.claude/rules/unity/ecs_patterns.md`'s no-system-to-system-calls rule — the same reasoning that already lets `CreateActionEffectSystem` call `ControlQuery`/`CountryRelations` methods inline.
- **No per-war dynamic card instance is needed** (unlike `stop_friendship`/`stop_rivalry`'s `RelationCardTarget`/`RelationCardSyncSystem` machinery): since a country can be in at most one war at a time (`Wars.IsInWar`'s existing guard), a single static config row per card, gated purely on numeric conditions, is enough to always mean "the war this country is currently in" — the same reasoning `Docs/Specs/26_07_29_00_decrease-enemy-control-card/spec.md` used to justify skipping dynamic instances for its own always-resolved-at-play-time targeting.
- **Unplayable reason:** add a new reason (e.g. `not_at_war`) surfaced when the failing condition expression involves `isInWar`, following the same substring-detection convention already used to map a `totalCountryControl`-involving failure to `no_enemy_control` (`Docs/Specs/26_07_29_00_decrease-enemy-control-card/spec.md`'s Tech Notes) — new locale key `action.country.unplayable.not_at_war`. The control/opinion/war-progress threshold failures fall through to whatever existing generic reason bucket already covers other advisor-gated cards' insufficient-control/opinion cases; exact wiring is a plan-time detail.
- **Locale keys** (placeholder, non-binding, following the project's existing card flavor-text convention): `action.ultimatum.name`/`.desc`, `action.surrender.name`/`.desc`, `effect.ultimatum_effect.name`/`.desc`, `effect.surrender_effect.name`/`.desc`, `game_log.war_resolved_format`, `action.country.unplayable.not_at_war`.
- **`ActionVisualConfig`** (`Assets/Scripts/Unity/Common/ActionVisualConfig.cs`, asset at `Assets/Configs/ActionVisualConfig.asset`): two new front-image entries keyed by `actionId: "ultimatum"` / `"surrender"` — placeholder art acceptable (art generation itself is out of scope, see below).
- **Bot AI mechanical update only:** `Game.Bots/BotObservation.cs`'s existing `ActionPlayability.Evaluate` call site needs whatever mechanical parameter update the shared signature already requires (it already takes an entity id per the `stop_friendship`/`stop_rivalry` work) but no new bot heuristic or strategy for deciding when to play either card is added — both are treated as interchangeable with every other card by existing bot decision-making.

## Out of Scope

- Any success/failure roll for either card — both always succeed once played, per the confirmed requirements.
- Any gameplay consequence of playing either card beyond ending the named war and recording who won/lost (no gold reward or penalty, no control/opinion change, no territory or relation change) — the issue specifies only the win/lose outcome, so nothing further is added; a richer consequence is future work if wanted.
- Any way to end a war besides the existing unconditional debug stop-war command and these two new cards — no additional peace-negotiation UI or mechanic is introduced.
- Changing `WarSystem`'s monthly decay mechanic, natural (non-card) war progress changes, natural war declaration, or allied/multi-country wars — all remain out of scope exactly as `Docs/Specs/26_07_25_06_war-mechanics-core/spec.md` left them.
- Any UI for the player to manually choose which of the two eligible countries (the selected one or its opponent) "wins" — the outcome is always fixed per card (Ultimatum → selected country wins; Surrender → selected country loses).
- Re-balancing the cost, thresholds, or mechanics of any other existing country card as a side effect of this feature.
- Card artwork generation — placeholder art acceptable, consistent with every prior country-card spec.
- Any cooldown or new pacing/throttling mechanic for either card.
- Opponent AI/bot strategy for deciding when to play "Ultimatum"/"Surrender" — only the mechanical `ActionPlayability.Evaluate` signature/context update is included, not a new heuristic.

## Ambiguities

Both points below were confirmed by the owner in the issue thread; resolutions kept here for the historical record.

- ~~"Own" war progress reading~~ — **Resolved:** the selected-country-relative signed reading (own progress ≥ threshold, defender's own progress = `-WarProgress.Value`) is correct as specified. The owner confirmed the current-mechanics limitation (Ultimatum's ≥50 gate being realistically defender-only until progress can be raised) is expected for now and will be addressed by a way to raise war progress, tracked separately in [issue #80](https://github.com/KonH/GlobalStrategy/issues/80).
- ~~Consequence beyond ending the war~~ — **Resolved:** no additional consequence (no gold/control/relation change) beyond ending the war and logging the outcome, as already written in this spec.
