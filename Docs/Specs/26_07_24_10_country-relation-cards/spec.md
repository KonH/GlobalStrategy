# Spec: Country Relation Cards

## Feature Intent

As a player, I want to play "Make Friend" and "Make Rival" action cards on a selected country, so that I have a direct, player-facing way to shape that country's diplomatic standing with other countries instead of relations only ever being seeded at game start or changed through debug tooling.

## Acceptance Criteria

Legend: `Precondition => Action => Outcome`, grouped under a shared precondition where one applies to several rows.

- The player selects a country and opens its Actions panel for the first time.
  - The country has no prior action-card history => the hand/deck are built as today => "Make Friend" and "Make Rival" join the existing country-card pool (alongside Sphere of Pressure, advisor cards, and Royal Audience) and are eligible to be dealt or drawn like any other country card, subject to their own eligibility conditions below.
- The selected country currently has at least one other available country that is neither its friend nor its rival ("a suitable candidate exists").
  - "Make Friend" is in hand and its cost/other conditions are met => the card is shown as playable.
  - "Make Rival" is in hand and its cost/other conditions are met => the card is shown as playable.
- The selected country currently has no suitable candidate (every other available country is already either its friend or its rival).
  - The deck/draw system evaluates "Make Friend" or "Make Rival" for a free hand slot => neither card is eligible to be drawn into the hand, mirroring how advisor/ruler cards are withheld below their control threshold.
  - A copy of "Make Friend" or "Make Rival" is already sitting in hand when the last suitable candidate disappears (e.g. because another card or a debug action just consumed it) => the card is shown as unplayable (greyed out), with a condition label explaining no suitable country remains. `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: is this "no suitable candidate" state actually reachable in practice — see Ambiguities — and should it grey out the card or should the card simply never leave the deck in the first place?]`
- The player plays "Make Friend" with a suitable candidate available and its cost is paid.
  - The play resolves => the system automatically picks one suitable candidate country and calls the existing relation model to set a Friend relation between the selected country and that pick, exactly as if the debug "Set friend" action had been used for that pair; the previous relation state for any other pair is untouched.
  - The played card leaves the hand and its cooldown begins, following the same post-play flow (vacated-slot redraw, cooldown gating) as every existing country card.
- The player plays "Make Rival" with a suitable candidate available and its cost is paid.
  - The play resolves => the system automatically picks one suitable candidate country and sets a Rival relation between the selected country and that pick, the same way "Make Friend" sets a Friend relation; the previous relation state for any other pair is untouched.
  - The played card leaves the hand and its cooldown begins, following the same post-play flow as every existing country card.
- Either card is played and (per the success-rate design still to be settled, see Ambiguities) a success roll is made.
  - The roll succeeds => the relation is set as described above.
  - The roll fails => no relation is set, but the card still leaves the hand and its cooldown still begins, exactly like a failed Sphere of Pressure / Letter of Commendation / Royal Audience play.
- The player hovers the success-rate percentage on either card, and/or either card is on cooldown in hand or in the deck pile.
  - The rate is dynamic (formula-based) => a breakdown tooltip appears, same as Letter of Commendation/Royal Audience; the rate is fixed => no tooltip appears, same as Sphere of Pressure. `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: fixed or dynamic rate — see Ambiguities]`
  - The card is on cooldown => the existing day/month/year cooldown label appears below the card name, identical in format to every other country card.
- The player has played "Make Friend" (or "Make Rival") and its cooldown is active, then opens the country's Actions panel again.
  - The other card ("Make Rival"/"Make Friend") is checked for playability => whether its own cooldown is independently tracked or shares a cooldown with its counterpart is a design decision still to be settled. `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: see Ambiguities]`

## Tech Notes

- New action definitions in `Assets/Configs/action_config.json`: `make_friend` and `make_rival`, both `ownerType: "country"`, following the same `ActionDefinition` shape as `sphere_of_pressure` (see `Docs/Specs/26_06_20_23_card-config-refactor/spec.md`). Each references a new effect via `effectIds`.
- New effect types in `src/Game.Configs/EffectConfig.cs`: add `SetCountryRelationEffectParams : ActionEffectDefinition` with a `Kind` field (`RelationKind`: `Friend`/`Rival`, from `src/Game.Components/CountryRelation.cs` / `GS.Game.Common`), registered in `ActionEffectDefinitionListConverter`'s switch (alongside `DiscoverCountry`/`ControlChange`/`OpinionModifier`) under an `effectType` string such as `"SetCountryRelation"`. Two effect entries (`make_friend_effect`, `make_rival_effect`) in `effect_config.json`, differing only by `Kind`.
- Candidate selection and relation application follow the same two-phase, marker-component pattern `DiscoverCountry` uses (per `.claude/rules/unity/ecs_patterns.md`'s no-system-to-system-calls rule), rather than resolving the pick inline in `CreateActionEffectSystem`:
  - `src/Game.Systems/CreateActionEffectSystem.cs`: extend the `foreach (var effectId in def.EffectIds)` dispatch (currently handling `DiscoverCountryEffectParams`/`ControlChangeEffectParams`/`OpinionModifierEffectParams`, lines 37-104) with an `else if (effectDef is SetCountryRelationEffectParams relationParams && !string.IsNullOrEmpty(countryId))` branch that creates a new marker entity carrying `{ EffectId, OrgId, CountryId (the selected/source country), Kind }` — mirroring the existing `DiscoverCountryEffect` marker creation at line 40-41.
  - New `src/Game.Systems/SetCountryRelationSystem.cs` (static, `Update(World world, Random rng)` signature mirroring `DiscoverCountrySystem.Update`), invoked from the top-level orchestrator (`GameLogic.Update`) alongside the existing `DiscoverCountrySystem.Update` call, not from inside another system. For each marker entity: build the candidate list from all `Country` entities where `IsAvailable` (per `CountryConfig`/seeded state) is true, excluding the source country itself and excluding any country for which `CountryRelations.GetRelation(world, sourceCountryId, candidateId)` is non-null (i.e. already friend or rival either way); pick one candidate (selection weighting is an open question, see Ambiguities) and call `CountryRelations.SetRelation(world, sourceCountryId, candidateId, kind)` (`src/Game.Systems/CountryRelations.cs`); destroy the marker entity afterward, matching `DiscoverCountrySystem`'s consumption pattern. Bump `CountryRelationsVersion` is already handled inside `SetRelation`'s callers per the country-relations spec — confirm `SetRelation` itself increments it (per `Docs/Specs/26_07_23_06_country-relations/spec.md`'s Tech Notes) so `VisualStateConverter` picks up the change without extra wiring here.
  - Game Log: emit a sibling event entity (e.g. `RelationSetApplied { OrgId, CountryId, TargetCountryId, Kind }`) after the relation is set, following the same "separate sibling entity, not attached to the effect component" convention `DiscoverCountrySystem` uses for `DiscoveryApplied` (see the ordering-note comment at `DiscoverCountrySystem.cs:97-98`), for Action Log UI consumption (`Docs/Specs/26_07_18_07_action-log-ui/`).
- Eligibility / "does a suitable candidate exist" check: the current `conditions` DSL (`src/Game.Configs/ExpressionNode.cs`, evaluated via `ExpressionContext { Control }` in `ActionPlayability.Evaluate` and `DrawCardSystem.DrawCountryCards`) only supports scalar `control`/`opinion` lookups — it has no existing node type that can express "at least one other available country has neither a friend nor a rival relation with me." Two implementation paths are viable and the choice affects both `ActionPlayability.cs` and `DrawCardSystem.cs`: (a) add a new boolean-returning DSL node type (e.g. `hasSuitableRelationTarget`) backed by a `World`-aware evaluation path (the current `ExpressionContext` has no `World`/`countryId` reference, so this would require threading those through), or (b) special-case `make_friend`/`make_rival` in `ActionPlayability.Evaluate` and `DrawCardSystem.DrawCountryCards` with a direct candidate-count check ahead of/instead of the generic `Conditions` loop, bypassing the DSL for just these two cards. Path (b) is more consistent with the codebase's existing pattern of keeping the DSL to pure scalar math and gating anything relation/discovery-shaped through dedicated code (c.f. how `DiscoverCountry`'s weighting lives in typed effect params rather than in a DSL node), but flag for the plan phase. `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: see Ambiguities]`
- Cooldown, hand/deck display, cooldown label formatting, dynamic success-rate tooltip: reuse the existing country-card infrastructure unchanged (`deckCopies`, `cooldownDays` on `ActionDefinition`; the cooldown label logic and tooltip breakdown UI already used by Sphere of Pressure/Letter of Commendation/Royal Audience) — no new UI code required beyond wiring the two new `actionId`s into the same rendering path, plus new name/desc locale keys and `ActionVisualConfig` entries (`Assets/Scripts/Unity/Common/ActionVisualConfig.cs`) for card art.
- `CountryConfig`/available-country check: candidate filtering must use the same `IsAvailable` gate already established by `Docs/Specs/26_07_23_06_country-relations/spec.md`'s seeding logic (`src/Game.Configs/CountryConfig.cs`, `CountryEntry.IsAvailable`) — currently 20 countries in `Assets/Configs/country_config.json`.

## Out of Scope

- Any UI to let the player manually choose the target country for either card — selection is always automatic, per the feature request.
- Any new relation kinds beyond the existing `Friend`/`Rival` (per `Docs/Specs/26_07_23_06_country-relations/spec.md`'s Out of Scope).
- Changes to the debug relation commands/menu (`DebugSetCountryRelationCommand`, `DebugClearCountryRelationCommand`, the "Selected country" debug menu) — they continue to exist unchanged alongside these new player-facing cards.
- Any gameplay effect of a relation once set (AI behavior changes, combat bonuses, event triggers tied to friend/rival status) — this feature only adds a player-facing way to set the same relation data the earlier spec already modeled; consuming that data elsewhere is future work.
- Cards targeting a specific chosen country's relation to a third specific country the player selects (e.g. "make Country A and Country B rivals" from a country not currently selected) — these cards always operate on the currently-selected country's own relations only.
- Opponent AI organisations playing these cards.
- Card artwork generation (placeholder art acceptable, consistent with the original country-action-cards spec).

## Ambiguities

- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Cost, success rate (fixed vs. dynamic-by-control formula), deck copy count, and cooldown duration for "Make Friend" and "Make Rival". Existing country cards range from free/50g/100g/200g cost, fixed-50% or `base% + control/N` dynamic rates, and 30/60/90-day cooldowns with 3 deck copies each — there is no established default for a diplomacy-flavoured card, and these are gameplay-balance numbers, not conventions to infer.]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Should either card require a minimum player-org control in the selected country to be eligible (mirroring the 10-control gate on advisor cards and 20-control gate on Royal Audience), or should they be available regardless of control, more like Sphere of Pressure's day-one availability?]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Should a success roll apply at all? Every existing country card has one (fixed or dynamic), but "Make Friend"/"Make Rival" are pure select-and-set actions with no numeric magnitude to scale a roll against, so an always-succeeds design is also plausible. If a roll applies, what drives the rate — flat value, or control/opinion-scaled like the advisor/ruler cards?]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Is candidate selection uniform-random among all eligible countries, or weighted — e.g. by proximity/distance the way `DiscoverCountrySystem` weights by `ProximityMapData` distance from the org's HQ, by the selected country's existing control level, or by how many friends/rivals each candidate already has (to spread relations out or concentrate them)?]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Is the candidate pool restricted to countries the player's org has already discovered (`DiscoveredCountry` per `src/Game.Components/DiscoveredCountry.cs`), or does it include all `IsAvailable` countries regardless of discovery state? The country-relations spec's historical seeding ignores discovery entirely, but a player-facing "make friend/rival" action might reasonably be limited to countries the player actually knows about.]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: What happens when zero suitable candidates exist for a country (every other available country is already friend or rival with it)? Options: the card is never dealt/drawn (needs a new eligibility mechanism per the Tech Notes DSL-gap entry above), the card is drawn but shown greyed out with an explanatory label, or the card is playable but resolves as a guaranteed no-op/failure. With ~20 available countries this is unlikely early on but becomes plausible late-game as more relations accumulate.]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Should "Make Friend" and "Make Rival" share one cooldown (since both act on the same country's "next relation slot") or track cooldowns fully independently, allowing both to be played in quick succession against two different candidates?]
- [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Card names and flavour text. Proposed placeholders following the existing 19th-century-diplomacy tone: "Make Friend" → "Cordial Accord" (*A treaty of friendship, sealed with warm words and mutual advantage — the first step toward a lasting partnership.*); "Make Rival" → "Declaration of Rivalry" (*A pointed rebuke, delivered through official channels — from this day, cooperation gives way to competition.*). These are suggestions only, not settled names.]
